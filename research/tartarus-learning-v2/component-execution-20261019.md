# Component debt connected to paper execution

The optional `ComponentPolicy` v7 / `sig_scoped_component_debt_v6` profile connects the [component ledger](component-debt-ledger-20261019.md) to actual simulated fills, original FIFO attribution, paid account/collateral reads, scoped HTTP acknowledgments and the growth reward. This is an accepted-fill funding bridge, **not complete ALL engine parity or a live-enabled lending strategy**. GPU017 and its already frozen018 successor remain unchanged.

The source under review is the saved official SIG OpenAPI SHA256 `fbdf23e54d317392f9fb4a659b9511a01fad065e7a384f28091ef04894a732c9`, especially `GET /account`, `GET /portfolio/collateral`, `POST /orders`, `PortfolioCollateral` and `AllExecutionEconomics`. No fresh live-schema verification is claimed. Original documentation: <https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/docs>.

## Execution and information

Before mutating FIFO for a fill, the adapter quotes its canonical funding transition using isolated provisional event storage. Own-component advance is drawn first, then spendable cash, then other components in an explicitly declared order. The accepted transition must reproduce that quote and agree with FIFO net positions. Borrowing and repayment change actual cash and component debt together; original FIFO realized profit remains separate. Complementary buys retain redemption credits. Negative cash is a liability, not spendable cash: buying power is `max(cash, 0) + unused eligible advance`.

Atomic basket rollback restores component positions, debt and immutable fill-event identities alongside original cash, FIFO, order history and liquidity. A sealed episode digest includes component cash, positions and debt. The wealth reward subtracts outstanding principal; event-level opportunity accounting also removes debt changes from fill cash flows. Borrowed principal cannot count as trading growth or independently profitable opportunities.

Paid `/account` observations expose cash and available advance. Paid collateral observations expose available advance plus component floors, outstanding debt, covered exchanges and relationship identities; they do not refresh cash or lots. The simulator uses internal revisions only to prevent older receipts from replacing newer baselines. HTTP order acknowledgments expose aggregate execution economics and the traded component's identity, without a private per-other-component draw breakdown. Extra component/economics feature rows are read-only; all legal policy actions and sampled fields remain available.

The optional experiment flag is `component_debt_accounting=true`, with `owned_order_pages`, joint local-memory density, scoped receipts and query-window/full-depth observations also enabled. The new architecture/protocol has the same parameter shapes but requires explicit `migrate_component_debt=true` for weight transfer, discarded old trajectories/scores and fresh optimizer state. Legacy profiles retain their original semantics.

## Evidence requirements and unfinished scope

Components default to **empty**. Actual recorded-market integration currently exercises that zero-component path; it does not invent linked collateral from cross-venue correlations or market prices. A nonempty `component_evidence` file must match its declared SHA256, have an availability timestamp no later than the paper world's current time, name a scope, and supply disjoint components with original SIG relationship IDs, explicit allowed binary joint outcomes and eligibility. A hash pins caller evidence; it does not authenticate the source or establish that the allowed-state list is exhaustive. Historical SIG topology and eligibility remain unverified for the current training corpus.

Nonempty components also require a complete explicit `other_draw_order`. The saved API does not specify priority among several other groups, so that order is a modeling hypothesis pending calibration, not an official-engine rule. Fees must be zero in this profile; unsupported fee accounting is rejected at configuration. The fresh-paper-order requirement prevents attaching this adapter to an existing order ledger or adopting live holdings.

This bridge still lacks changing relationship graphs, evaluation-budget eligibility transitions, lender calibration, complete resting-order resize/cancellation on falling buying power, settlement/refund/release/write-off and complete official valuation. Exact expiry/reporting assumptions from the owned-order profile remain. These gaps prevent a claim of full unrestricted production API equivalence, even though raw action support is unchanged. Multi-day three-venue coverage and independent profitable ROI are also unfinished.

## Verification

Fourteen focused tests cover zero-component differential accounting against the original FIFO engine, real accepted linked fills, debt-neutral wealth/opportunity rewards, floor-drop repayment, complementary redemption, paid scoped/stale observations, aggregate ACK economics, negative-cash affordability, no zero-power bootstrapping from a hypothetical future floor, topology pins/time boundaries, migration/PPO/checkpoints, atomic rollback and sealed-episode replay with outstanding debt. Linked outcomes and initial accounts in those fixtures are synthetic, not observed collateral calibration or market-profit evidence.

The complete learning-actions suite passed **275 tests in63.757seconds**. Actual recorded-data factory integration used two H32 policies/two worlds/two cycles:1,123 decisions and42 accepted updates. One candidate's worst marked selection ROI was-3.114%; the other recorded cases were0%, with only seconds of development coverage. These are plumbing results, not profitable strategy evidence. A separate actual v6-to-v7 experiment preserved every parameter tensor in both transferred founders, began with empty optimizer state, and completed560 decisions/20 updates. All113 runtime source pins were rechecked after both runs. [Exact results and migration evidence](component-execution-recorded-002.json).

The first recorded trial is retained but predates a source correction: the saved `/portfolio/collateral` contract supplies aggregate `availableAdvance` as well as component rows. The final adapter and focused test expose both through that paid read; they do not withhold documented information or reveal cash/positions through it. Final-source verification uses recorded trial002. No actual linked component was supplied to either recorded-data run; synthetic fixtures remain separate.

All experiments are offline: no account client, production activation, STOP, risk cap or financial ledger is changed. The [completed paired-return-method comparison](paired-return-method-002-review.md) also remains negative and does not justify switching the main learner's return method.
