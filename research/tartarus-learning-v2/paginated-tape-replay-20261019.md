# Paid, causal SIG trade-history pages

This is a checked simulator improvement for the next training source. The active phase015 process remains on its immutable `8e07f6452c446e345b5ca319342d5b91c5443230` source. It has not loaded these changes, and was not restarted. No account client, production control or financial state changed.

The earlier raw adapter removed and delivered the entire pending trade backlog for one read. A large backlog could exceed SIG's documented maximum200 rows. It also created a trade-read target only after a hidden print arrived, revealing activity before the model paid for it. The earlier phase015 evaluations retain this limitation; their results are not relabeled as results of the repaired simulator.

The new adapter opens a causal query from recorded prints available at request admission, delivers a maximum200 rows per charged read after the modeled latency, and preserves the rest behind opaque continuation cursors. Default direct reads request50 rows. The actor chooses any page size1–200 through its quantity latent; its side head selects continuation or a fresh query. Both choices have their correct PPO probability/entropy terms. Query metadata masks survive padded batched inference and fused recurrent PPO. An unfinished cursor retains its original snapshot when newer trades or late reports arrive. Repeating a cursor repeats the same facts; distinct source match IDs are never counted twice as flow.

Trade-read targets are declared for every eligible exchange before prints arrive. Only paid receipts publish page metadata, raw print tokens and flow statistics. Raw rows expose normalized YES price, exact-source quantity encoded for the network, original execution/receipt ages and exchange identity. They remain observation tokens rather than order targets. The latest paid page remains visible; previous pages are available to recurrent memory or another query. This does not restrict raw observations to the existing60-second flow aggregates. Earlier recorded prints within the same chronological role are also queryable before the episode warm-up; seeding that hidden history does not fill orders or deliver free observations. Cross-role history remains excluded.
