# Paid historical references during training

The frozen034 fit interval predates the live external-reference captures in its replay. Historical Kalshi prints and Polymarket samples were available through explicit actor reads, but the automatic data service collected only live `reference` records. A real fit-world audit at 20 minutes into October15 found causal values for all234 registered Kalshi targets and316 Polymarket targets, yet automatic collection made zero external requests in ten simulated seconds. This is an information-delivery mismatch, not evidence that historical sources were absent or that every old policy ignored them.

The new optional `model_pausable_history_reference_service_v3` collects registered external histories as well as live references. Background Kalshi requests ask for the newest single print; ordinary actor requests retain their original full page. Polymarket delivers its newest causal sample. Both use the same existing20/30 reads-per-second admission, source-availability clocks and delayed receipt machinery as explicit reads. Historical values remain nonexecutable information with their original provenance and ages. Distinct live-reference and historical-outcome identities are retained. The model can pause collection, request more history, choose its own actions or wait; SIG foreground priority and budgets are unchanged.

On the identical ten-second fit sample, V3 made201 Kalshi and295 Polymarket admissions, with488 delivered source-target observations by the endpoint. The201 count includes both endpoints of the ten-second interval; rolling one-second limits remain enforced. The original16 SIG reads were unchanged. This is a data-path check, not a learned strategy comparison or ROI claim. Some historical prints are old; receipt freshness does not make their source prices current executable quotes.

All43 focused checks pass, covering original profiles, shared limits, delayed immutable receipts, complete actor history access, model pause/resume, foreground priority, historical tensor delivery and checkpoint profile binding. The real-source audits used CPU only, existing fit bounds and the protected data-use registry. No independent data or production account was touched.

Current034 continues on its frozen source. The previously queued035 cooperative continuation also remains unchanged as of this publication. V3 is implemented and verified but has not yet trained or qualified a profitable model.
