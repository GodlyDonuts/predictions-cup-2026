# Optional full-episode return training — October19,2026

The active016 streaming PPO trainer uses bootstrapped compute chunks. Those chunks do not limit legal decisions or rewards, and bootstrapping can propagate longer-horizon value. They can nevertheless make delayed economic outcomes difficult to credit when the critic is inaccurate. Full-episode Monte Carlo targets are an optional comparison, not a proven cure or new reward. The [original PPO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347) separates rollout collection from minibatch optimization; the method below implements that separation with this simulator's existing reward and causal source controls.

Enable `closed_episode_return_replay:true` in the experiment configuration. The default remainsfalse and actual GPU016 remains on its frozen streaming source. The optional path:

1. Holds behavior weights fixed while all counterfactual TRAIN episodes finish. No optimizer updates occur during that collection. It leaves all API families, decision frequency, quantity, price and capital domains unchanged.
2. Writes actions, exact old probabilities/values, learn/reset/terminal flags, clocks/rewards and recurrent chunk boundaries to a compact SQLite log. It stores feature hashes instead of whole multi-day feature matrices. The record count may grow with the episode; in-memory compute chunks remain bounded. No decision count cap is introduced.
3. Seals only after every decision and entire economic episode is recorded, all ledgers are closed and recorded rewards reconcile to the original score. Full undiscounted returns include the original terminal correction for the human's consistency/evidence formula when that qualified ledger applies. Partial-history development remains explicitly development.
4. Reconstructs observations/actions against the same immutable source and original episode clocks. Every observation tensor hash, step reward/clock and chunk financial/liquidity hash must match. Source fixtures verify mismatches after accepted early updates restore the entire learner and optimizer. Open or changed logs, behavior mutation and evolution/test capture are refused.
5. Uses the original rollout probabilities, full-return targets and cohort-wide advantage normalization for clipped PPO minibatches. A session verifies original actor weights and tracks subsequent authorized student changes. The ordinary streaming fresh-policy guard remains in force outside this explicit sealed-episode path. KL rejection ends cohort optimization. Recurrent backpropagation remains chunked with stored behavior hidden state; this is not unlimited-gradient or unlimited-memory learning.

An accepted update during reconstruction is logged as provisional; committed counts appear only after the cohort optimization returns successfully. A late replay error restores the CPU-backed learner/optimizer snapshot. The source log is immutable and is not a financial-world restart or live account client. Further minibatch epochs reuse that same closed TRAIN rollout, never new or held-out observations.
