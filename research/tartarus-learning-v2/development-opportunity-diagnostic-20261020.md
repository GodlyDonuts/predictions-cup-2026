# Why the short development screens favor inactivity

A source-pinned scan of the existing, already exposed development books found just two positive one-share aggressive round-trip opportunities in the first 120-second selection screen and one in the second. These are exchange-side counts, not independent events; the first two are opposite sides of exchange983. The median observed spread is0.005. The entire50-minute exposed evolution calendar contains30 exchange-sides with a positive hindsight round trip. [Exact scan and results](development-opportunity-diagnostic-20261020.json), [runner](development-opportunity-diagnostic-20261020.py).

The diagnostic allows hindsight and ignores request admission, gaps, queue, impact and adverse selection, while requiring at least2.5seconds between purchase and sale and one displayed share. It is an optimistic feasibility bound for this particular crossing strategy, not a causal policy or a limit on passive/ALL/portfolio strategies. It does not show that every other strategy lacks edge. It never accesses an independent test.

GPU021's first completed eight-member generation had a flat winner and seven negative selection rewards. The flat winner made zero fills in all four screen/stress cases and mostly chose reads and alarms. A representative losing candidate made79fills in its first nominal case and lost16.43% of marked wealth. Selecting the highest reward was working; changing selection to promote a worse loss would not fix the lack of useful experience or selection duration.

The replacement development experiment removes short selection truncation, uses one continuous registered50-minute evolution window under nominal and adverse execution, and extends training paths from two to ten minutes. A smaller recurrent model is measured separately to permit longer experience without retaining the old58.2M-parameter cost. No action-family prohibition, trading reward bonus, forced capital deployment or future price input is introduced. This remains development work, not the requested complete daily-growth historical run. [Actual replacement and remaining limits](horizon-throughput-022-20261020.md).
