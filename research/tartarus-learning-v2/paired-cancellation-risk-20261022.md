# Why the retained two-NO screen is conditional

Checked October22 against the official [settlement guide](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/settlement-and-payouts) and [trading guide](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/markets-and-trading). Winning shares pay1 and losing shares0. Cancellation/N/A refunds the refundable cost of the remaining holding. Opposite-side netting within one market pays the paired amount immediately. These are different mechanisms; two related contracts do not automatically net against one another.

The [retained18:24 book screen](conditional-no-pair-screen-20261022.json) includes1038/1039: Democratic and Republican party-winner contracts for Wisconsin House District1. Their original SIG roots were independently reread and hash-checked from the retained registry; both identify race64672, General stage99980, electionNovember3,2026, with different winner parties. This establishes the configured common event, not a venue-enforced joint settlement guarantee or current executable prices.

The table adds cancellation stress instead of treating that ordinary assumption as a guarantee. The last two rows are conservative asymmetric-cancellation stresses. The checked participant guides do not establish that independently administered related contracts must always share a cancellation result. They are not predictions that these outcomes will occur. A true unconditional payout floor needs that missing guarantee or separate explicit coverage; ordinary mutual exclusion alone does not provide it.

This changes how the retained opportunity should be valued: it can be an ordinary-event hedge with a conditional surplus, but cannot be treated as immediate collateral, borrowed buying power or a guaranteed150 profit. Existing holdings' actual refundable basis must be used in any production valuation. No new account requests, orders or simulated profits were generated by this analysis.

A reviewed conditional trade can still be evaluated under the existing human risk authority, with explicit exception assumptions, actual cash funding and the original execution checks. The missing guarantee changes its valuation rather than establishing a new blanket prohibition.
