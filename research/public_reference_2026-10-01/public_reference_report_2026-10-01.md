## Settlement and mapping differences that matter

- **Kalshi party contracts:** Congress is the source agency. Ordinarily the first certificate-presenting person sworn in for the specified term determines the winning party; a replacement before seating can matter. A party change between election day and swearing-in uses the election-day party. Accelerated resolution may occur when at least four of eight named media sources agree, with none contradictory. Thus certified winner, inaugurated winner, and election-day party winner are not universally interchangeable. [Full Senate rules](https://assets.kalshi.com/contract_terms/SENATEPARTY.pdf)
- **Kalshi Alaska person contract:** The primary criterion is Mary Peltola winning the 2026 Alaska Senate election. The API secondary rule uses the winner under the applicable electoral system who takes office, or whose scheduled inauguration passes without a different candidate taking office. It resolves from official certification/declaration; disputes use the final certified result or highest competent court, with a hierarchical source fallback for competing claims. A postponed election can remain open until rescheduled or two years after the original date; annulment after announcement but before taking office can leave it open for a rerun or expiration. This is a different contract family from SENATEPARTY, and is not an exact substitute merely because Peltola is the only listed Democrat. [Person-contract rules in official event response](https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/KXAKSENATE-26NOV03)
- **Alaska RCV:** The official November 3, 2026 sample ballot lists Mary Peltola as registered Democrat, Gerald Heikes and incumbent Dan S. Sullivan as registered Republican, and separate Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. Peltola is the only listed Democratic candidate, but there is a write-in option. Party affiliation is explicitly not a nomination or endorsement. Final ranked-choice outcome, not first-round plurality or aggregate Republican first-choice share, is the relevant election winner. The state says RCV rounds begin after day-15 counting and results remain unofficial until certification. [Official ballot](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election/2026/General/SampleBallots/Federal%20only), [RCV rules](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/election-information/)
- **Fusion / independent / replacement risk:** A candidate may not map one-to-one to a party contract. Never sum two cross-endorsement or fusion-party claims for the same candidate as mutually exclusive outcomes. Do not classify an independent as Democratic merely because of prior affiliation or caucusing. The retrieved Kalshi Senate rules do not provide an explicit fusion-line tie-breaker; if relevant to a game contract, require its exact definition. The Alaska party/person 1-cent difference is not automatically arbitrage.
- **Polymarket:** Its indexed NC rules cover the 2026 election including runoffs, define party representation by nomination, exclude independents from D/R, and use AP/Fox/NBC agreement or official certification. That differs from Kalshi's party/seating/replacement language. [NC reference](https://polymarket.com/event/north-carolina-senate-election-winner?marketSlug=will-the-republicans-win-the-north-carolina-senate-race-in-2026&outcomeIndex=0)

## Simulation fair-value hypotheses and use

1. **Reference anchors, not independent fair values:** Midpoints are NC 94.15, FL 9.75, AK party 68.5, RI 99.0, MA 98.65 cents. Use the entire bid/ask band and depth, plus a settlement/model-uncertainty buffer. A game ask materially below the lower end is a research candidate to buy; a game bid materially above the upper end is a research candidate to reduce or sell. No game book was inspected in this assignment, so no current game mispricing is established.
2. **FL deserves prompt re-underwriting:** A bullish simulation holding priced materially above 10 needs independent evidence to justify its disagreement with the present 9.7/9.8 reference. Do not average away a mismatch in candidate, primary versus general election, or special versus regular election.
3. **AK requires a larger evidence buffer:** The deeper party quote is 68/69, not the 73 public-page headline. Price final RCV, distinguish the two Sullivans, and cross-check any polling model on final-round assumptions. Peltola and party outcomes are highly related, not separate independent forecasts.
4. **MA/RI are finite-upside capital decisions:** Even if a game quote is cheap relative to 98–99, tie-up, fill depth and alternative opportunities matter. Thin reference levels and no RI turnover weaken confidence in tiny apparent edges.
5. **Avoid duplicate evidence:** Kalshi/Polymarket trader prices incorporate many of the same polls and news as election models. A model explicitly using market odds is circular evidence when averaged with these markets; use its odds-free variant if documented. Even a polls-only model shares inputs, so agreement is not two independent confirmations. Related [holdings research](../holdings_forecast_evidence_2026-10-01.md) and [new-race research](../new_edges_2026-10-01.md) report that DDHQ uses 25% market input for these five Senate races and that VoteHub Complete is also market-aware. Neither is independent corroboration of these market quotes; use an explicitly odds-free model variant or underlying polling evidence instead. This methodology finding comes from that prior research and was not independently reverified in this reference-market assignment.
6. **Faster safe refresh:** The documented Kalshi public market and orderbook GETs can refresh this small watchlist without accounts. Record observation time, HTTP Date, bid/ask/size, explicit last price, and game book at comparable times; revalidate contract mapping if names/rules change. Market metadata updated_time and the event's zero-valued last_updated_ts are not quote timestamps. Follow documented rate limits; do not add authentication or real-money execution.

[Kalshi official public-data quick start](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/quick_start_market_data) explains the unauthenticated endpoints and bid-only orderbook convention. Raw timestamped API JSON snapshots supporting the report are saved under [raw/](raw/); the [publication manifest](public_reference_manifest_2026-10-01.json) retains every original source hash, source URL and timestamp, and explicitly identifies the omitted broad discovery inventory. No API trade endpoint was called.
