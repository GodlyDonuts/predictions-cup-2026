## Economic change

The existing profitable inventory SELL path scales only the best bid quantity.
With `inventoryExitDepthBps=5000`, a one-share best bid produces zero shares,
even when deeper prices also clear the unchanged worst-lot cost, entry fee,
exit fee and `inventoryMinimumGain`. Existing `inventoryTakerAfterMs=0` already
permits immediate profitable exits; this change introduces no faster loss timer.

After explicit `profitableDepthMode:true` in pinned Control, the sole worker
records `ordinary_profitable_depth_mode_v1`. Future normal planning on an
unmanaged, nonpackage owned exchange sums only consecutive own-side executable
levels that individually satisfy the original per-share gain formula. It takes
50% of that qualifying depth, capped by the original quantity/owned/pledge and
opposite-BUY constraints. The limit is the exact marginal price covering that
quantity; lower-price shares never receive the best price. NO depth uses each
original YES ask's complement. The gain and fees are checked again at the
selected marginal limit. No average-price profit assertion is used.

Synthetic example: 1 share at .705 plus 999 at .700, with worst owned basis
.690 and zero fees, yields a 500-share .700 limit, rather than zero shares from
the original one-share top level. This is a proposal example, not an observed
production fill or expected-profit forecast. Prices/size can change before
execution; the unchanged fresh physical preflight and actual fills govern cash.

If the existing top-level profitable-age condition is false, the maximum-age
loss path is unchanged, including its top-depth fraction and limit. When that
condition is true, the new profit-preserving path can also apply after maximum
age; it retains the original age reason and expiry semantics. Insufficient
profitable depth falls back to the original branch. Managed forecast scopes
and protected packages keep their original behavior. Maker BUY sizing is
unchanged.
