## Price math and maximum bounds

- Buy-YES lead difference = public YES bid − Cup reported YES best ask.
- Buy-NO lead difference = public NO bid − (1 − same-contract Cup YES best bid). Cup NO ask derivation uses the [official shared-book trading mechanics](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/markets-and-trading), supported by the saved verified documentation. It infers no depth or quantity.
- Prices are parsed from the original JSON with Python Decimal, and every subtraction/sum uses exact decimal arithmetic. Latest-trade and card prices are never substituted for ask quotes.
- Maximum nominal winning payoff gain = 1 − Cup ask before fees; loss if the selected side loses = Cup ask. Neither is an expected return. The optional public-ask-minus-Cup-ask upper-band field is only a quote-band difference, not an executable resale gap or probability bound.
- A zero public bid quantity suppresses the gap rank. Unknown quantities remain explicitly unknown. No fee, slippage, fillability, payout-basis or cancellation adjustment is silently assumed to be zero.
