## Independently checked legal sources

[K.S.A.25-306](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/025_000_0000_chapter/025_003_0000_article/025_003_0006_section/025_003_0006_k/) limits acceptance to one nomination for the same office. [25-306e](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/025_000_0000_chapter/025_003_0000_article/025_003_0006e_section/025_003_0006e_k/) allows nominations to be received from multiple parties, but requires one designation to be selected and treats the other nominations as declined. [25-613](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/025_000_0000_chapter/025_006_0000_article/025_006_0013_section/025_006_0013_k/) connects the printed party designation to nomination papers and ordinarily prints a candidate once for that office. Received nominations, accepted nominations, ballot labels and outside endorsements are different facts.

The [Secretary of State's candidate information](https://www.sos.ks.gov/elections/candidate-information.html) confirms major-party primary nomination, minor-party convention/caucus nomination (Libertarian/United Kansas), and independent petition routes. It is a procedure source, not the final candidate roster.
