# Tournament-wide activity discovery

After verified transport recovery, phase61 used one private tournament subscription over a freshly validated237-exchange catalog. It completed16 serialized REST receipts under the existing five-read budget and captured5,046 pushed books and174 unique native trades. No gaps, truncation, schema errors or unknown markets were recorded. Mean observed trade delivery lag was4.438seconds and the maximum29.777seconds. This supports actual broad observation; it does not establish a fill, profitable signal or uniformly low latency.

`research/stream-activity.mjs` pins the stream stats sequence, verifies every source row hash and predecessor, deduplicates native trades and rejects conflicting facts or future execution clocks. It measures execution volume over the last three minutes, current displayed queues/spread and book source age. Books older than ten seconds and markets with observed gaps/resync requests are excluded from the eligible activity ranking. This ranking uses past flow and current queues; it is not a fill probability or complete trade-window guarantee. The current snapshot is `data/moloch-stream-activity-20261014-61.json`.

The terminal ranking includes exchange922 as a two-tick-spread candidate with four recent trades/1,609shares and displayed queues809/94. This market previously contributed losses to the small historical wallet. Its activity is a reason to test current quoting, not to discard that loss evidence. A source-fresh, funded forward comparison is required; no archived quote is submitted or assumed executable. Shutdown delay also ages final book observations, so a terminal ranking cannot substitute for live scoped reconciliation.
