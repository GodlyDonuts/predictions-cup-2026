# Inventory-cycle diagnostic and coverage limits

Twelve continuous funded 100,000 paper comparisons replay episodes 18–28 at 1%, 10% and 100% participation, with maximum holding ages of one, three, ten and thirty minutes. All use original acquisition execution timestamps and permit later depth-dependent break-even exits without an additional ten-minute wait. Source snapshots, predecessor links, complete journal chains and original baseline financial parity are verified.

At 1% participation, every age case realizes zero and retains 4.320 cost. At 10%, every age case realizes zero and retains 44.640. Full participation realizes −9.585 and retains 140.480. No holding-age setting establishes profitable closed turnover. This does not prove the age policies are economically equivalent: later counterfactual inventory lacks required execution coverage.
