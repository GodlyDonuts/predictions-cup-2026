# Market-relative dip research

`node live-paper/relative-dip-audit.mjs` verifies existing root and market-price hash chains, builds receipt-causal features, and saves a historical diagnostic under `data/relative-dip-live-study.json`. It makes no network calls and cannot authorize orders or fills.

The common factor is the median signed five-minute change in clipped log odds from at least twelve other races. Explicit Democratic/Republican cached titles provide orientation only. Each race counts once; the target race and its opposing party contract are excluded. A fixed unit loading removes the common move from the target's change. This loading is a hypothesis, not a calibrated model. Both reference/current quotes have bounded receipt age, latency and spread. Historical recovery references exclude the current and prior minute and require thirty earlier minute observations. Repeated polls do not add independent observations.

Two fixed diagnostic cohorts compare a prior lower-quartile bid discount of at least 0.02 with the subset also having residual log-odds change below −0.05. Shared first episodes have a thirty-minute race cooldown. Chronological historical periods purge fifteen-minute labels crossing their boundary. Historical analysis designed after data exists remains development evidence, even when a chronological split is used.

Five/fifteen-minute labels use the first subsequent quote, with a sixty-second observation-delay bound. Missing, slow, crossed or unusable first observations remain missing; later favorable observations cannot replace them. Decimal micro-unit arithmetic keeps exact zero recoveries from becoming floating-point wins. Gross bid minus entry ask and a separate two-tick latency-buffer scenario are reported. Added fees are zero. Bulk quotes supply no depth or source timestamp, so these values are not simulated fills or executable portfolio returns.

Freeze a new future experiment once with `node live-paper/relative-dip-audit.mjs --freeze relative-dip-20261002-v1`. This creates an immutable definition and digest in the restricted runtime, pinning policy, code, identity bank, source prefixes and a new cutoff. It preserves every prior paper bank and wallet. Read subsequent evidence with `node live-paper/relative-dip-audit.mjs --cohort relative-dip-20261002-v1`. Each replay verifies the frozen prefixes and rejects changed code/identity/policy. Older observations only warm the feature history; they cannot become prospective episodes. Do not overwrite a definition to retrain or tune. A new hypothesis needs a new named cohort and later cutoff.
