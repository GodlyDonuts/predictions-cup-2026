# Paper position observation

The observer wrapper publishes a receipt-aware companion's paper read focus, bound to its live lock nonce, complete stats hash, wrapper identity and latest original journal checkpoint. The collector accepts only a fresh (20 seconds), open, healthy cohort with the same canonical market universe. STOP, changed ownership, incomplete checkpoints or mismatched projections remove the optional focus.

Held paper instruments are mapped through the frozen universe. The frozen report omits pending map keys: buy candidates come from causal signals at the pending intent's actual creation receipt; coincident receipts may retain several candidates. Sell intents are covered by held instruments. This selects observations only.

At most two existing optional discovery/comparison/edge-observation slots are replaced. Official managed or held risk, original edge trial deadlines, reserved comparison and basket/package reads remain protected. Excluded account markets are skipped. No extra read slots, writes, entry authority or processes are introduced. Fees and frozen wallet accounting are unchanged.
