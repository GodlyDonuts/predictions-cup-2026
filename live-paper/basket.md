# Dual-NO basket measurement

This independent observer measures the combined cost of NO positions in two metadata-matched election contracts. If their YES predicates are mutually exclusive, at least one NO pays one SUSQie under normal binary settlement. The contracts need not exhaust all outcomes: a third-party winner could make both NO legs win. Matching metadata alone does not establish that relationship.

The conditional floor does not cover voids, refunds or overrides. The [official settlement guide](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/settlement-and-payouts) describes cost-basis refunds. Likewise, the [multi-leg API](https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/docs) provides atomic placement without guaranteeing equal or simultaneous fills. This observer never submits orders, credits hypothetical fills or reports P&L. Even a verified normal-resolution relationship remains distinct from an unconditional payoff guarantee.

The frozen cohort records exact market/exchange/predicate fingerprints, a source-journal prefix, a future cutoff, policy and code hashes. It walks the observed YES bid ladders to obtain NO offer costs, adds one adverse tick per leg, limits each level to 1% of displayed quantity, and caps a measured package at 25 SUSQies. Both books need fresh authoritative REST receipts within 20 seconds. Later independent changes to both books after the latency boundary are necessary to count persistence; repeated unchanged snapshots do not establish reusable liquidity.

Fresh quotes from one scoped bulk response can request a paired-depth measurement every fourth collector cycle. Both legs fit within the existing four-book limit. Official obligations, managed paper positions and selected official candidates retain their reads. Account exclusions apply before any cached pair can be evaluated. New measurement legs do not gain official entry eligibility. Missing, failed or stale observer state leaves the existing book plan intact.

When this cohort is configured, collector startup captures the tournament-scoped relationship list through its existing serialized client, shared pacing and persistent backoff. Pagination is bounded to ten pages and incomplete capture is explicit. An empty list is evidence of an empty scoped response, not proof that metadata pairs have a particular payoff. Captured relationships do not mutate an active frozen cohort or authorize an order.

Start the first cohort with `Start-Basket.ps1 -Initialize -KeepSession`. Later starts use `Start-Basket.ps1 -KeepSession`. The local `/api/basket` endpoint on port 8787 exposes observations and uncertainty; `node live-paper/fleet-health.mjs --pretty` checks the worker. `BASKET_STOP`, runtime `STOP`, and workspace `STOP` are honored. Runtime files remain in the restricted LOCALAPPDATA directory, including an append-only observation journal. Restart replays that journal against the original source records and checks all state and observation hashes before processing new records. Never replace an existing bank or journal to change parameters; use a separately identified successor cohort.
