# Execution-aware online paper cohort

This separately named cohort learns conditional five-minute **padded bid minus actual modeled purchase price**, instead of assuming the historical midpoint will return. Its six fixed categories combine top-level queue imbalance and spread. It prepares a one-share training trial no more than once per market per six minutes. Entry requires a later independent quote whose request starts at least one second after the decision, one percent usable depth and a price within the original limit. The exit label uses the first usable subsequent quote requested after the full horizon, with a sixty-second lateness allowance. Missing, interrupted and unfilled trials are counted; they are never zero-profit fills. Training labels include losses and one adverse tick on both executed sides. Fees are zero.

The trading gate uses only labels already matured at the current decision. It needs 25 labels across at least three markets in the same category, with its empirical lower quartile at least 0.01 SUSQ per share. Keep the latest 256 completed labels per category. This is a fixed empirical hypothesis, **not** an independence-adjusted confidence interval: markets/common events can correlate, pooling can be inappropriate and distributions can change. Model hashes and quote/source identities are recorded with decisions. Trial labels are conditional on hypothetical fills and do not establish actual profitability.

Risk: one share per entry, at most three occupied markets, 25 SUSQ aggregate attributed basis, 100 SUSQ lifetime cumulative buys without recycling sale proceeds, fixed 0.02 stop and five-minute holding horizon. The reused executor requires subsequent independent depth and applies latency, partial-fill accounting and latched risk exits. Actual consumer time timestamps new intents. No old request can fill an intent created after that request. Source/session barriers revoke buys/trials while retaining held lots; stale marks are withheld.

`node live-paper/executable-edge-watch.mjs --name <new-cohort> --initialize` creates a new frozen algorithm/config bank, verifies the sanitized publication prefix, warms labels without wallet trades, and then consumes the existing sole collector. Resume the same name without `--initialize`. Global/workspace STOP, EXECUTABLE_EDGE_STOP and the cohort STOP block operation. Each cohort has its own exclusive worker lock, immutable bank and fsynced hash-chain checkpoints; restart replays original consumer times and checks every state hash. Partial or changed journals/code/source fail closed, preserving evidence. No credential is loaded, no API traffic is added and no contest order is submitted.

Source history is read-only. The worker currently verifies the full source prefix on each read; this is intentionally conservative at the observed local file size. The unfilled/missing-label counts and usable category coverage must accompany performance results. Correctness tests and development labels do not justify activation or scaling. Evaluate new wallet outcomes only from observations after its recorded cutoff, without resetting losses or selecting only favorable categories afterward.
