## Scope and identity

One state covers one exact account tag, tournament, market, exchange and explicitly verified source namespace/epoch. `Engine{epoch}` and `Legacy{epoch}` cannot merge. Epoch assignment must come from independently verified source/cutover evidence; the API's bare numeric ID does not establish it. The module refuses mismatching source/scope inputs and preserves the receipt reference for reconciliation.

Within that fixed namespace, identity is `(physical order ID, trade ID)`, not trade ID alone. Both counterparties can share a trade identifier. Side, action, quantity, own-outcome exact price, explicit fee and original-precision execution clock remain immutable facts. Equivalent clock offsets normalize to the same nanosecond timestamp. A conflicting identity retains both fact versions and their private receipt references instead of replacing admitted accounting.

The pure typed input is **not a raw receipt validator**. The integration caller must verify hashes against original private bytes, authenticated scope, original durable intent and canonical order identity, signed quantities, zero-fee policy or explicit fee evidence, complete pagination, exact totals and no ambiguous source epoch. Caller-supplied hashes/assertions are not independently authenticated by this module.

## Observation, eligibility and financial admission are separate

`observe` checks a full cumulative tape, deduplicates unchanged facts, and retains newly observed facts in staging. A newer cumulative tape must still contain previously observed facts for the same order. Missing historical fills, conflicting identities, incomplete pages, scope errors and source changes make eligibility fail closed. They do not manufacture a cancellation or release any liability. Original raw receipts must already be durably retained; invalid normalization leaves their reference and a reconciliation issue.

`plan` returns staged fills in oldest-first order plus reasons. Every plan has `execution_authority=false`. It becomes *eligible for review* only when a trusted dispatcher supplies a closed-roster claim covering all relevant owned orders on this exchange:

- new submissions are frozen under one unchanged dispatcher generation;
- there are no unknown attempts or foreign/unbound exposure;
- every binding has a matching independent closed-order receipt;
- its complete fill tape started at or after that terminal receipt, and its exact total equals the closed-order filled quantity within admitted order size;
- there are no unresolved staged issues or missing roster members.

These are **caller assertions**, not proof generated by the module. The integration must independently verify them from the sole worker's durable dispatch journal, actual receipts and current ownership/control state. A stale or incomplete supplied roster can still lie about omitted orders; a Boolean cannot operate a cross-device lock. Such uncertainty blocks review eligibility at the caller. Active orders remain staged; a projection checkpoint does not promote them to terminal.

Distinct-order execution timestamp ties are conservatively ambiguous, irrespective of numeric trade-ID order. Unequal-price ties within an order are also ambiguous. No arbitrary lexical order is represented as economic proof. This may block opportunities; it is intentionally conservative until primary sequence/cutover semantics support a stronger proof.

## Late fills and restart

The state separately records observed and financially admitted facts. Its frontier is the latest exact execution timestamp among admitted fills on this exchange. An unseen fill at or before that frontier is retained in staging and raises `UNSEEN_FILL_AT_OR_BEFORE_ADMITTED_FRONTIER`. This is conservative even for equal-price/same-side events. Eligibility stays blocked across restart; it is not a filter that suppresses valid official accounting events.

An actual late fill means the ledger requires explicit reconciliation. Continue collecting source evidence through the existing worker; block new affected-exchange economic execution while the execution owner reconciles physical holdings, exact cash and FIFO basis. Do not keep trading an inconsistent wallet, discard the official event, reorder an immutable prefix, reset lots or clear unknown acceptance to make checks pass. Unrelated execution policy remains the parent's decision; this module cannot stop or start the service.

Checkpoint serialization includes admitted identities, observed facts, pending conflicts/issues, latest receipt references, explicit source epoch, frontier and exact native bank sequence/hash. A checksum catches accidental changes; it is **not an authenticity signature**. `restore` requires the caller's independently verified expected bank anchor and validates the frontier against admitted facts. Store the checkpoint privately and atomically with fsync/parent-directory durability; these I/O responsibilities intentionally remain outside the pure module.

`record_admitted` only constructs a prospective next checkpoint after a reviewable plan. It rejects a changed prepared state, modified fill facts, blocked plans or non-advancing bank sequence for new fills. The caller must first verify that the exact original receipt-linked financial events are already in the immutable native prefix. This helper neither writes that bank nor proves the claimed anchor. Do not persist the proposed frontier before actual bank acceptance.

## Integration boundaries requiring another checked change

1. Reconstruct staging from the existing immutable bank and exact official source receipts, not from a new empty state at a nonempty bank anchor. Audit the historical FIFO/source namespace first. A checkpoint hash alone cannot certify a fabricated bootstrap. Do not silently relabel current equal-price production profit as incorrect or rewrite history.
2. Durably stage raw receipt references and normalized facts before native admission. Freeze the relevant owned-order roster before gathering closed terminal/tape proofs; revalidate the generation afterward. Account and dispatch unknown states remain authoritative. Keep individual known-order GET plus complete later tape as terminal liability proof.
3. When all relevant orders are genuinely closed, assemble and review the complete sorted cohort. Existing native bank writes are individual events, so a write-ahead mapping must reconcile crash recovery between partially appended events and the staging checkpoint. Replay exact original facts, retain native duplicate/conflict protection, and advance only the verified prefix frontier. Do not assert a batch transaction that the bank does not implement.
4. A previously admitted event older than a later unseen fill cannot be made FIFO-safe merely by sorting new input. Route that situation into explicit reconciliation/migration with preserved old/new source proofs. Sticky issues are intentionally not cleared by this module; an operator-approved successor state requires its own audit.
5. A closed-roster gate can reduce execution availability and is unsuitable as a drop-in replacement for active-order financial reconciliation. Until a stronger common barrier is documented and checked, do not enable new opposing/package strategies that depend on streaming cross-order FIFO correctness. Keep baskets disabled and do not infer authority from `eligible_for_review`.
