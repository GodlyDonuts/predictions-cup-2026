# Median production trial

The human explicitly authorized a small official trial on October 6, followed by continued paper research and checked production improvements. This is an exploratory activation, not a declaration of profitability. The frozen one-tick paper case had two +0.005 SUSQie closes; its subsequent two-hour exit lost 0.010, leaving realized P&L at zero at 06:45 UTC. An additional position remained open.

The official controller uses the frozen paper reference: prior-hour bid history, at least 20 retained observations spanning 30 minutes, excludes the newest eligible bulk point, and requires an ask at least one tick below the upper median. Bulk prices screen candidates; independent depth-20 receipts establish executable proposals. Each fixed-limit one-share proposal requires a later independent book after at least three seconds, fresh scoped account reconciliation, and sufficient individual-level depth under the 1% cap. Actual official fills establish cost and ownership.

The pilot permits at most 1 SUSQie per order, 10 aggregate attributed cost, 25 cumulative purchase cost, three managed markets, zero fees, and six hours of new entries. Existing attributed exits and uncertain-order reconciliation continue after the entry window. Unlike paper proposals, official orders expire after 30 seconds; expiry/cancellation must reconcile before replacement. Stops do not guarantee a loss bound.

The existing collector remains the only account client. Read-only preflight uses its serialized queue, complete paginated orders/fills, a newest-fills recheck, positions, open orders and cash. A new same-owner activation retains the original ownership record, binds the human instruction and exact private reconciliation receipt, and requires fresh observations. No Daenerys release is invented.

Paper experiments retain their original banks and accounting. Improvements require checked source revisions and preserved attribution; paper gains are not presented as official gains. Runtime activation and actual orders must be verified separately from passing tests or writing configuration.
