# Expired maximum-age exit as a fresh-read hint

Task [#137](https://github.com/GodlyDonuts/predictions-cup-2026/issues/137), Viserion/exit-refresh-20261015. Scope: NEW `moloch/live-runtime/src/exit_refresh.rs` and this document. Root retains worker/library integration, account access, publication, and production. This helper performs no I/O, native mutation, intent creation, order submission, or activation renewal.

## Problem and bounded proposal

Root observed expired native maximum-age SELL drafts and no prefetch starts while the ordinary worker completed owned reconciliation and seven snapshot reads. A quote originally living about13s (including3s activation) can expire before those reads finish. This observation identifies a scheduling problem; it does not prove an exit is profitable or that its former price/depth remains executable.

An expired, never-attempted quote may identify a held scope for one bounded read packet. Its order ID, size, price, expiry and old book never authorize a replacement. The existing sole worker reads tournament, positions, open orders, exact account cash, market, nodes, then book LAST. Normal source/session acceptance of that book may produce a new native exit. Only a unique new native quote, with its own original activation and expiry, can proceed through the ordinary authority and durable dispatch pipeline. Nothing extends or reuses an old intent.

`inventoryAON` is a native strategy label. The physical POST `/orders` has no all-or-none mode here. An accepted SELL can partially fill and leave a resting liability. Preserve ordinary full fill tapes, exact terminal proof, cancel/fill race handling and immutable dispatch history. Neither native retirement nor absence from an open-list receipt closes a physical order.

## Helper API and invariants

- `ReadHint::from_expired(quote, safety, now)` accepts only an expired maximum-age `inventoryAON` SELL with zero native filled quantity, valid original clocks/price, current owned inventory and no intent, attempt, unknown, STOP or stream failure. A retired quote is permitted only as this read hint. Any non-null cancellation denies it. Old size need not equal current size: this is a scope hint only.
- `RefreshFacts::new(scope, hint, [Receipt;7], now)` binds the exact seven original receipts and scope to a private SHA256 digest. Order is tournament/positions/openOrders/exactCash/market/nodes/book. Endpoint strings, HTTP200, chronological request/receive order, covered open orders, contexts and target exchange are checked. No receipt slot or timestamp can be replaced under the same plan. Account receipts retain20s and market/nodes/book10s age limits from ORIGINAL request starts and receives. The target book's original `asOf.at`/sequence are additionally checked. This is an additional gate, not full receipt semantic validation; existing SDK and ProductionAuthority still validate supported contracts, complete position/account reconciliation, exact cash, depth and intent.
- Before ordinary cleanup starts this refresh flow, record maximum native qid and flow-start time. Normal cleanup may create the successor before the seven reads. After fresh-book acceptance reread native records. `RefreshPlan::select` requires exactly one matching new qid greater than the pre-flow maximum and different from the expired hint, created inside the whole flow through book acceptance. Same market/exchange/side SELL/maxAge/style, zero filled, no retirement/cancel, original activation delay at most3s, and available current inventory are required. A changed new native price or quantity is allowed; both are frozen into the new plan. Two eligible candidates are ambiguous and denied.
- `BookAcceptance::new` additionally requires the ACTUAL normalized book event successfully durably admitted by Session, its native committed-head SHA256 and acceptance clocks. It checks the admitted book against ordinary `receipt_events` normalization of the exact last receipt, including original sequence/source/request/receive clocks and full depth. A cached different book, invalid/recovery-blocked book, gap, duplicate-only/no actual admission, or failed source/session acceptance cannot certify the flow. The caller must supply actual committed evidence, never construct a second normalized event and pretend it was applied. This addresses a specific hazard: `RestSource::accept` may emit `invalid:true` during recovery while a precreated successor stays uncanceled until native minimum order age. HTTP200 and an uncanceled quote alone do not prove valid fresh-book application.
- `Safety` is mandatory at every gate. `same_exchange_pending_physical_order` must be true for ANY attempted/ACKed order on the exchange without authoritative closure, either side or action, even if native expired/retired or absent from an open list. This blocks replacing a partially filled/resting SELL and prevents a pending BUY changing inventory during the wait. Unknown acceptance anywhere and unresolved attempts also block. `reserved_sell_quantity` is other same-side native/physical SELL reservation excluding this candidate; unknown reservation coverage must deny rather than assume zero. Current available quantity is owned minus reservations.
- `Safety.lifecycle_fingerprint` must be a nonzero SHA256 digest derived from the current bank identity, effective policy, owned economic lifecycle and durable dispatch head. Include adoption/fill/terminal/intent/attempt/ACK/cancel changes and bank/policy replacements. Exclude mere clock ticks so waiting itself does not invalidate the plan. The worker must derive it from authoritative state, never a constant or a guessed timestamp. Any change after selection invalidates this plan, including equal net holdings with different fills. Other-market dispatch changes can conservatively invalidate it too.
- `Safety.native_head_fingerprint` must equal the exact committed native head captured by `BookAcceptance` (hash the current committed sequence/chain head; `Session::manifest_hash()` identifies the original manifest and is NOT the changing head). Any later native event, even an otherwise harmless clock tick, invalidates this short-lived plan. The brief wait therefore services STOP/health without applying other source events under the same plan; if control/reconciliation work changes the bank head, abort and rebuild later. This conservative condition is separate from the economic-lifecycle fingerprint and prevents unobserved gap/source/policy changes from being hidden by an unchanged quote.
- `RefreshPlan::stage(current_quote, same_facts, scope, current_safety, now)` compares the entire frozen new quote, scope, receipt digest and lifecycle fingerprint, then rechecks original receipt/book ages, coverage and native timing. It returns `WaitUntil(originalActivation)` only within3s, or `Ready` after activation with more than3s remaining original expiry. No sleep, new timestamp, new fact substitution, old key, or permit is generated by the helper. Missing/changed state is an error, never a fallback to the expired quote.

## Exact worker integration boundary

1. Keep the existing serialized account worker, limiter, STOP, unknown handling and owned full-tape reconciliation. Bound refresh snapshots with the existing one-preflight-per-pass permit and fair scope cursor. Deduplicate old hints by market/exchange/side; never run one packet for each historical expired quote.
2. Gather current old-hint safety facts from the durable dispatch log and queue plus actual owned/reserved state. Require no same-exchange physical liabilities without closure. Capture all seven snapshots with the book last. No new intent or physical attempt occurs in this stage.
3. Feed the actual book through the ordinary `RestSource` and durable Session. Do not parse a REST book ahead of previously parsed but unadmitted native events. Capture the actual valid matching admitted book and committed native head ONLY after all emitted events have been successfully applied. Select the fresh native quote using the real pre-cleanup maxqid/flow start and book-acceptance certificate. Do not invent a gap, alter clocks, force retirement of a valid draft, or reset policy to manufacture a fresh candidate.
4. Retain the plan, original SnapshotSet and exact seven-receipt binding in memory for the brief original activation wait. Yield cooperatively to STOP/health and normal owned reconciliation; avoid busy waiting or blocking all control processing. Refresh authoritative safety/lifecycle and reread the same native quote on every step. Any changed lifecycle, stale original facts, cancel, fill or replaced qid aborts; a later pass needs a completely new read packet and new plan. No automatic retry of attempted writes.
5. Only after `Ready`, construct **all** Order/spec/permit/idempotency bindings from `plan.quote()` under its NEW qid and original expiry. Recheck current quote and plan after any queue wait and at the physical authorization boundary. Existing ProductionAuthority, fresh account attribution, exact cash, contract eligibility, size/depth, shared limiter, native funding, durable intent and unknown gates remain mandatory. The helper does not authorize trading. Build/record the new intent only once these checks pass; partial/resting actual orders remain pending until ordinary authoritative reconciliation closes them.

Native behavior: every `advance`, including never-submitted retirement, can itself replan from the cached book. The helper permits this distinct successor created during ordinary cleanup if the exact fresh valid book is actually admitted afterward, the native after-state retains the same successor without cancel/retirement, and all original timing/liability/authority conditions pass. It never allows the expired hint itself, a quote predating the pre-flow maxqid, or a replacement manufactured by artificial retirement. The flow still need not produce any eligible successor; denial is an ordinary outcome.
