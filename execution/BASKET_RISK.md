# Basket risk preview

`basket-risk.mjs` is an offline dry-run calculator. It does not construct executable order payloads, access accounts, submit orders or establish settlement proof. Original prospective observers and their pinned code are unchanged.

Each NO leg may fill at its highest allowed limit. Reserve quantity times that limit per leg; a ladder-weighted quoted cost cannot replace the hard-limit reservation. The exact integer planner validates segment counts, monotonic leg limits, cumulative cost and conditional margin before selecting a quantity. Total package budget, each unmatched leg's principal and minimum hard-limit unit margin are separate constraints. Defaults are 25 SUSQ total, 5 SUSQ per unmatched leg and 0.005 unit conditional margin. These are preview constraints, not a production risk authorization.

The observation is historical and cannot authorize a current order.

The stress table covers equal fills, either leg alone and unequal quantities. Under consistent mutually exclusive normal YES predicates, the combined NO payout floor is the smaller filled quantity. A third-party winner can make both NO legs win. One refunded contract and one losing NO leg can still lose principal; both refunds return cost, while inconsistent overrides or failed predicate assumptions can lose the full package. Report the conditional and unconditional bounds separately.

The [official trading guide](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/markets-and-trading) explains YES/NO complements and opposite-position netting. The [settlement guide](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/settlement-and-payouts) describes cost-basis refunds and administrator overrides. The checked-in official API guide describes atomic multi-leg placement, which does not establish equal fills. A future executor must reconcile returned leg/order IDs and actual side/quantity/cost, fence uncertain acknowledgments, and cap or unwind exposure from partial fills. This calculator supplies none of that authority.
